    List your app

    A realm lists itself: deploying it is the proof that you control it. New apps open New, then stay in their category; those of registered names come back through Rediscover.

    Not showing up? Your deploy transaction carries a StoreRegisterRejected event with the reason.

    Know Go? You already know most of Gno. Read the docs

    A package cannot call into a realm, so its namespace owner lists it with one transaction.

    Listed packages appear in Build and count toward Top builders.
